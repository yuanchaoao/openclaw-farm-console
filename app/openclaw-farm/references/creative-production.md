# Remote production

Remote generation uses the user's configured model and may incur charges. Connection does not authorize publishing, sharing or unrelated model calls.

Use independent jobs/sessions, generation/review/acceptance artifacts and hashes. Verify actual quality and files, preserve approved templates and gates. Receipt/prose alone is insufficient.
